# Travel and Expense Policy

## What can be claimed

Employees can claim reasonable costs incurred for company business: travel, accommodation, meals while travelling, client entertainment approved in advance, and work equipment under 500 SAR.

## Limits

- Hotel: up to 600 SAR per night in Saudi Arabia and up to 150 EUR per night in Europe.
- Meals while travelling: up to 200 SAR (or 50 EUR) per day.
- Flights: economy class for flights under 6 hours; business class needs approval from the department head.

## How to claim

Submit claims in the finance portal within 30 days of the expense, with a receipt for every item above 50 SAR. Your manager approves the claim, then Finance pays it in the next monthly payroll run.

Claims submitted after 30 days need written approval from Finance and may be rejected.
