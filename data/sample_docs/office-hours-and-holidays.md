# Office Hours and Public Holidays

## Core hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time. Outside core hours, employees may arrange their schedule with their manager as long as they work 40 hours per week.

The Riyadh office is open 08:00 to 18:00, Sunday to Thursday. The Lisbon office is open 08:30 to 18:30, Monday to Friday. Badge access outside these hours must be requested from the office manager at least one working day in advance.

## Public holidays

Each office observes the public holidays of its country. Riyadh observes Eid al-Fitr (4 working days), Eid al-Adha (4 working days), Founding Day and Saudi National Day. Lisbon observes the Portuguese national holidays.

If a public holiday falls on a weekend, it is not moved to a working day unless the government announces a substitute day.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week. Fully remote arrangements need approval from the department head and People Operations.
