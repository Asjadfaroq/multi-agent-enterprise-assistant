# Customer Support SLA

Customer Success answers support tickets according to the customer's plan and the ticket priority.

## Priorities

- **P1 – Service down**: customers cannot create or send invoices. First response within 1 hour, 24/7, for all plans.
- **P2 – Major issue**: a core feature is broken but a workaround exists. First response within 4 business hours.
- **P3 – Minor issue or question**: first response within 1 business day for Growth and Enterprise, 2 business days for Starter.

## Escalation

If a P1 ticket is not resolved within 4 hours, the support lead escalates it to the on-call engineer and informs the customer's account manager. Enterprise customers receive a written incident summary within 3 business days of resolution.

## Ticket hygiene

Every ticket must have a priority, a category (`billing`, `bug`, `account`, `it` or `question`) and an owner. Tickets waiting on the customer for more than 7 days are closed automatically with a note explaining how to reopen them.
