# Company Overview

Larkspur Systems builds Ledgerly, an invoicing and payments product for small businesses. The company was founded in 2019 and has 64 employees across two offices: Riyadh (headquarters) and Lisbon.

## Teams

- **Product Engineering** builds and operates Ledgerly. It is led by the VP of Engineering.
- **Customer Success** handles onboarding, support tickets and renewals.
- **People Operations** owns hiring, payroll, benefits and employee relations.
- **Finance** owns billing, expenses and vendor payments.

## How we work

Most teams work Sunday to Thursday. Lisbon staff follow a Monday to Friday week and overlap with Riyadh on Monday to Thursday afternoons. Company-wide announcements are posted in the #announcements channel and summarised in the monthly all-hands meeting, held on the first Tuesday of each month.

## Getting help

For IT problems, open a ticket in the internal tickets system with the category `it`. For questions about a policy, check the knowledge base first, then ask People Operations.
