# Disciplinary Procedure

This procedure applies to conduct issues. Performance issues follow the performance review process instead.

## Stages

1. **Informal conversation**: the manager raises the issue privately and records a short note.
2. **Written warning**: issued by the manager with People Operations present. It stays on file for 6 months.
3. **Final written warning**: issued by the department head. It stays on file for 12 months.
4. **Dismissal**: decided by the department head and the Head of People Operations together.

## Gross misconduct

Theft, fraud, harassment, violence, and deliberately sharing confidential customer data are gross misconduct. They may lead to dismissal without earlier warnings after an investigation.

## Rights of the employee

The employee is told the allegation in writing before any meeting, may bring a colleague to formal meetings, and may appeal any formal decision within 10 working days to a manager who was not involved.
