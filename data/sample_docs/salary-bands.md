# Salary Bands 2026

These bands apply to Riyadh-based roles and are reviewed every January. Figures are monthly base salary in SAR, before housing and transport allowances.

| Level | Title examples | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Associate, Junior Engineer | 9,000 | 11,500 | 14,000 |
| L2 | Engineer, Specialist | 13,000 | 17,000 | 21,000 |
| L3 | Senior Engineer, Senior Specialist | 19,000 | 24,500 | 30,000 |
| L4 | Staff Engineer, Team Lead | 27,000 | 33,500 | 40,000 |
| L5 | Principal, Head of Department | 36,000 | 44,000 | 52,000 |

Housing allowance is 25% of base salary and transport allowance is 10% of base salary.

Offers above the midpoint need approval from the department head. Offers above the maximum need approval from the CEO and People Operations. Individual salaries are confidential and must not be shared outside People Operations and the employee's management chain.
