# Performance Review Process

## Cycle

Performance reviews run twice a year: a mid-year check-in in June and a full review in December. Only the December review affects compensation.

## Steps

The employee writes a self-assessment against their goals. The manager collects feedback from at least 2 peers. The manager writes the review and proposes a rating. A calibration meeting per department compares ratings across teams before they are final.

## Ratings

- **5 – Exceptional**: well beyond expectations. Expected for at most 10% of employees.
- **4 – Exceeds expectations.**
- **3 – Meets expectations.**
- **2 – Partially meets expectations**: triggers a development plan.
- **1 – Does not meet expectations**: triggers a performance improvement plan (PIP) of 60 days.

## Compensation

Employees rated 4 or 5 are eligible for a salary increase of 5% to 12% and a bonus of up to 15% of annual base salary. Employees rated 3 are eligible for an increase of up to 4%. Ratings of 1 or 2 do not receive an increase that year.
