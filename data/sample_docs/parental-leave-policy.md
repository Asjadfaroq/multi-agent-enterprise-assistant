# Parental Leave Policy

## Maternity leave

Birthing parents receive 14 weeks of fully paid maternity leave. Up to 4 weeks may be taken before the expected due date. An additional 4 weeks of unpaid leave may be requested.

## Paternity and partner leave

Non-birthing parents receive 10 working days of fully paid leave, to be taken within 3 months of the birth or adoption.

## Adoption

Adopting parents receive the same leave as birthing or non-birthing parents, starting from the date the child joins the family.

## Returning to work

Employees returning from maternity leave may work reduced hours (6 hours per day) at full pay for the first 4 weeks after their return. Requests for longer flexible arrangements go to the manager and People Operations.

## How to apply

Notify your manager and People Operations at least 8 weeks before the planned start date, or as soon as possible for adoptions.
