# Ledgerly Product FAQ

## What plans does Ledgerly offer?

Ledgerly has three plans. **Starter** costs 49 SAR per month and includes up to 50 invoices per month and one user. **Growth** costs 149 SAR per month and includes unlimited invoices and up to 5 users. **Enterprise** is priced per contract and adds single sign-on, an audit log and a dedicated account manager.

## Which payment methods are supported?

Customers can collect payments through mada, Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay and bank transfer. SADAD support is planned but not yet available.

## Is Ledgerly compliant with e-invoicing rules?

Yes. Ledgerly generates ZATCA-compliant e-invoices (Phase 2) with a QR code and cryptographic stamp for customers in Saudi Arabia.

## Can customers export their data?

Customers can export invoices and payments as CSV or PDF from the Reports page at any time. A full account export is available on request through support and is delivered within 5 working days.

## Is there a free trial?

Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial. No card is required for the Starter and Growth trials.
